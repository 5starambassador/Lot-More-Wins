## Description (4000 max)

Lot More Wins Partner gives you savings at every participating outlet, plus rewards when you bring friends along.

SAVE ON EVERY BILL
Show your Personal Discount QR at billing in any Lot More partner outlet and the discount is applied straight away. No coupons and no codes to remember.

SHARE AND EARN REWARDS
Share your Referral QR with family and friends. They get a discount on their bill, and you earn referral points every time they close a bill with your QR.

REFER AND WIN
Track your successful referrals on the home screen. Reach the referral goal and you unlock a special discount on your next purchase.

DISCOVER PARTNER OUTLETS
Browse cafes, restaurants and stores in the Lot More network. See the discount each outlet offers, view photos and opening hours, and call, email or get directions in one tap.

YOUR WALLET
See your points balance and your full history of discounts, referral earnings and rewards in one place.

STAY IN THE LOOP
Get notified about new outlets, festive offers and the rewards you earn.

Sign in securely with a one-time code. No password to remember.
